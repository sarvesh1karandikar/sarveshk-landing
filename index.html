03 / Engineering approach

Plan for the
unhappy path.

I’m interested in the point where a system’s design meets its behavior: who owns the state, how work is coordinated, and what happens when a process goes away.

  1. Draw the boundaries.

    Keep runtime isolation and orchestration responsibilities explicit.

  2. Understand the state.

    Treat session lifecycle and concurrency as first-class design concerns.

  3. Test the interruption.

    Use controlled disruption to explore behavior beyond the happy path.
